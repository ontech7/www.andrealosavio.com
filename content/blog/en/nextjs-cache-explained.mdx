## Why the cache looks broken

The first time I watched a `revalidate: 60` get ignored for hours, I was sure I
had found a bug. I had not.

```ts title="app/page.tsx"
export const revalidate = 60;
```

## The layers that matter

A request passes through more than one cache on its way in, and each one plays
by its own rules.
